# CISO-EX-014 — GDPR compliance assessment

**Platform:** CISO Assistant | **Time:** 15 min | **CISSP:** 1, 2

---

## Objective

Map Cadre Corp's GDPR compliance controls in CISO Assistant. Link data processing activities, DPIA requirements, breach notification procedures, and cross-border transfer safeguards to specific controls.

---

## Steps

### Step 1 — Map GDPR articles to controls
| GDPR Requirement | Cadre Corp Control / Evidence |
|-----------------|------------------------------|
| Art. 5 — Data minimization | R-EX-043 data minimization principles, targeted collection SOP |
| Art. 30 — Records of Processing | RoPA document per EU engagement template |
| Art. 32 — Security of processing | CTL-EVIDENCE-ACCESS, CTL-COC, encryption at rest/transit |
| Art. 33 — Breach notification (72h) | IR playbook notification procedure, RISK-017 |
| Art. 35 — DPIA | R-EX-023 DPIA template, EuroMfg case study |
| Art. 44-49 — Cross-border transfers | SCCs + TIA for US storage, sub-processor disclosure |

### Step 2 — Create GDPR compliance dashboard
In CISO Assistant, create a GDPR-specific compliance view showing: (a) controls mapped to each article, (b) evidence status (documented/tested/audited), (c) gap articles (no controls mapped).

### Step 3 — Simulate a regulatory inquiry
"If a EU supervisory authority requests your Art. 30 RoPA within 30 days, can you produce it?" Verify the RoPA template is complete, current, and retrievable. This is the practical test of GDPR readiness.

---

## Interview Talking Points

1. **"GDPR compliance is provable, not assumed":** "When a regulator asks for our Records of Processing, I don't write one from scratch — I pull the template, updated with the current engagement, and provide it within hours. GDPR readiness means having the evidence organized before they ask."
