# VW-EX-010 — AI governance dashboard and reporting

**Platform:** VerifyWise | **Time:** 15 min | **CISSP:** 1, 7

---

## Objective

Navigate VerifyWise's AI governance dashboard, interpret AI-specific risk visualizations, and generate an AI risk report for executive stakeholders.

---

## Steps

### Step 1 — Navigate the AI governance dashboard
Review:
- **AI Risk Heatmap:** Where do Cadre Corp's AI risks cluster?
- **Project Risk Summary:** Bar chart — which project has the most AI risks?
- **EU AI Act Distribution:** Pie chart — how many projects per risk tier?
- **Mitigation Status:** What percentage of AI risk mitigations are implemented vs planned?

### Step 2 — Generate AI governance report
Export an executive summary covering: AI system inventory (3 projects, 3 models), EU AI Act classification distribution (1 High, 2 Limited), top AI risks (prompt injection, model supply chain), and mitigation status (67% implemented, 33% in progress).

### Step 3 — Cross-reference with CISO Assistant
Compare VerifyWise's AI risk view with CISO Assistant's overall risk view. The AI risks should appear in both platforms with consistent scoring — demonstrating unified risk visibility across classic GRC and AI governance.

---

## Interview Talking Points

1. **"AI governance has its own dashboard — but feeds the unified risk view":** "VerifyWise gives me the AI-specific view: which models, which risk tiers, which mitigations. CISO Assistant gives me the unified enterprise view. Together, they provide complete risk visibility — classic and AI — without duplication."
