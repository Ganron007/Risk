# VW-EX-007 — NIST AI RMF integration

**Platform:** VerifyWise  
**Time:** 15 minutes  
**CISSP Domains:** 1, 6

---

## Objective

Map Cadre Corp's AI governance to the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0) functions — Govern, Map, Measure, Manage. Demonstrate understanding of the US counterpart to the EU AI Act.

---

## Steps

### Step 1 — Map to NIST AI RMF functions
In VerifyWise, map Cadre Corp AI controls to the four RMF functions:

| RMF Function | Cadre Corp Implementation |
|-------------|--------------------------|
| **Govern** | AI governance in risk appetite statement, CISO ownership of AI risk, ISO 42001 certification target |
| **Map** | AI system inventory (VW-EX-002), model inventory (VW-EX-003), EU AI Act classification (VW-EX-004) |
| **Measure** | AI risk assessments (VW-EX-005), prompt injection testing, model output monitoring, classification drift tracking |
| **Manage** | HITL approval gate (CTL-HITL), model version tracking, incident response for AI findings, risk acceptance documentation |

### Step 2 — Contrast NIST AI RMF with EU AI Act
- **EU AI Act:** Regulatory, prescriptive, risk-tier-based, compliance-focused, EU jurisdiction
- **NIST AI RMF:** Voluntary, flexible, function-based, risk-management-focused, US-origin but globally applicable
- **Cadre Corp strategy:** Use ISO 42001 as the unifying framework, map to both EU AI Act (compliance) and NIST AI RMF (best practice)

---

## Interview Talking Points

1. **"I navigate both EU and US AI frameworks":** "The EU AI Act tells me what's legally required. The NIST AI RMF tells me what's best practice. I map Cadre Corp's AI governance to both — satisfying European regulators AND demonstrating to US clients that we follow a recognized risk management framework."
