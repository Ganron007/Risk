# VW-EX-013 — AI governance reporting for executives

**Platform:** VerifyWise | **Time:** 15 min | **CISSP:** 1, 7

---

## Objective

Generate an AI governance executive report from VerifyWise. Extract AI-specific metrics, risk summaries, and compliance status for board presentation.

---

## Steps

### Step 1 — Extract AI governance metrics
- **AI Systems Inventory:** 3 projects, 3 models, 2 providers
- **EU AI Act Distribution:** 1 High Risk, 2 Limited Risk, 0 Prohibited
- **Risk Summary:** 3 AI-specific risks identified, 2 mitigated, 1 partially mitigated
- **Framework Coverage:** ISO 42001 = 78% mapped, NIST AI RMF = 85% mapped
- **HITL Coverage:** 100% of AI systems with operational outputs have HITL approval gates

### Step 2 — Frame for the board
"Three AI systems deployed. One classified High Risk (RevAI — generates security findings). All three have documented risk assessments, model cards, and human oversight. ISO 42001 certification target: Q3 2027. EU AI Act compliance: on track for enforcement deadlines."

### Step 3 — Export the report
Generate PDF from VerifyWise. Combine with the CISO Assistant board memo for a unified technology + AI governance board package.
