# INT-EX-003 — Unified risk register consolidation

**Platforms:** CISO Assistant + VerifyWise | **Time:** 15 min | **CISSP:** 1, 2

---

## Objective

Create a unified, cross-platform risk view that consolidates classic GRC risks (18 from CISO Assistant) and AI-specific risks (6 from VerifyWise) into a single board-ready risk posture summary.

---

## Steps

### Step 1 — Inventory all risks across platforms
- **CISO Assistant:** 18 risks (AD, evidence, SOC, cloud, lab infrastructure)
- **VerifyWise:** 6 AI-specific risks (3 RevAI + 2 systemic + 1 cross-project)
- **Overlap:** 3 risks appear in both platforms (RISK-013, RISK-018, RISK-012)

### Step 2 — Deduplicate and consolidate
Remove duplicates. Total unique risks: 18 (CISO) + 6 (VW) − 3 (overlap) = 21 unique risks.
Document the overlap: "RISK-013 (Prompt injection) is tracked in both platforms — CISO Assistant for enterprise risk, VerifyWise for AI-specific controls."

### Step 3 — Generate unified board view
Create a single-page summary: "Cadre Corp manages 21 unique risks across 2 GRC platforms. 89% actively mitigated. 3 risks overlap platforms — consistent scoring confirmed. AI risks represent 29% of total risk inventory."

---

## Interview Talking Points

1. **"Unified risk view — not platform silos":** "The board doesn't care which platform a risk lives in. They care about the total risk picture. I consolidate CISO Assistant and VerifyWise into one unified view: 21 risks, 89% mitigated, AI represents 29% of our risk landscape. That's the answer to 'what's our risk posture?'"
